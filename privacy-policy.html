SPORTY

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Sporty is an app for booking sports courts and finding games in Pakistan, starting in Karachi. This page says what we keep about you, why, and what you can do about it. "We" means Sporty.

In short

  • We never sell your data, and there are no ads.
  • A venue sees your name and contact phone only when you book it.
  • We never keep a record of where you are.
  • You can download your data or delete your account at any time.

What we keep

We keep only what the app needs to work:

  • Your name, and the email address Google or Apple gives us when you sign in. Apple can give us a hidden address instead of your real one.
  • A contact phone number, if you add one, so a venue can reach you about a game.
  • A profile picture, if you add one.
  • Your bookings: the venue, court, time, price, status and check-in, and the transfer reference if you send an advance.
  • Games you join, teams you are in, and the messages you send in game and team chats.
  • Reviews you write, venues you save, and plans you have at a venue.
  • Your points and badges.
  • A push token for your phone, so we can send you notifications.

If you register a venue

We also keep the venue details you send: its name, address, a pin on the map, sports, courts, prices, opening hours and photos, and your name and phone number. Our team calls you to check the venue, and keeps notes about that check. Only you and our team can see your request.

Your location

The app uses your location on your phone to find courts near you. To find them, it sends the place with the search, and our server forgets it as soon as it answers. We do not keep a record of where you are or where you go.

When you pick a place, the app saves that place on your phone. Searching for a place uses your phone's own maps service.

You can turn location off in your phone's settings. You can still pick a place by hand.

How we use it

We use your data to:

  • make and manage your bookings with venues,
  • show your games, teams and chats to the people you play with,
  • send you notifications about your bookings and games,
  • keep the app safe, and stop abuse and fake bookings,
  • find and fix crashes, and make the app better.

Who can see what

  • Other players see your name, picture, points, the teams you are in, your reviews, and your messages in chats you share with them.
  • A venue sees your name, contact phone and booking details when you book it, so it can run your game and reach you if something changes.
  • Our team can see your account when it needs to: to help you, to check a report, or to keep the app safe.

Services that help us run Sporty

These companies work for us and use your data only to run their part of the app:

  • Supabase keeps our database, sign in and photos. Its servers are in Singapore.
  • Google Firebase sends notifications to your phone.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report when the app stops working. It holds your phone model, system version and what the app was doing. It does not hold your messages.
  • Firebase Analytics tells us how the app is used, like which pages are opened most. We do not use it for ads.
  • Google and Apple handle signing in. We never see your Google or Apple password.

We never sell your data

We do not sell your data to anyone. We do not show ads, and we do not share your data for ads. We share data only as this page says, or when the law makes us.

Payments

For now you pay at the venue. Some venues ask for an advance. You send it to the venue yourself, and type the transfer reference in the app so the venue can check it. We never see your card or bank details.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, picture, contact phone, your place in teams and games, saved venues, plans, points and notification tokens. Your future bookings are cancelled. Past bookings stay with the venue for its records, with no name on them. Messages and reviews you wrote stay where they are, with no name on them, so chats and venue ratings still make sense.

Firebase keeps crash reports and usage data for a limited time, then deletes them.

Your choices

  • Change your name, phone number or picture: Profile, then tap your picture.
  • Get a copy of your data: Profile, then Download my data.
  • Stop notifications: your phone's settings, or Profile, Settings, Notifications.
  • Delete your account: Profile, then Settings, then Delete my account. You can also read how on our website.

Children

Sporty is not for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Keeping your data safe

Your data travels over encrypted connections. Our server checks every request, so a person can only see and change what they are allowed to. Only the people in our team who need it can see your account.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we change the date at the top. If a change is big, we tell you in the app first.
