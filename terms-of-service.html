Terms of use

Last updated 4 November 2026

These terms are the rules for using Sporty, an app for booking sports courts and finding games in Pakistan, starting in Karachi. By using Sporty you agree to them. "We" means Sporty. "Venue" means a place with courts that lists them on Sporty.

In short

  • Your booking is with the venue. Sporty helps you make it.
  • For now you pay at the venue.
  • Cancel for free until the time your booking shows.
  • Be kind in chats and reviews. Play safely.

Your account

You sign in with your Google or Apple account. Keep your phone and that account safe, because anyone using them can use your Sporty account.

You must be at least 13 years old to use Sporty. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should agree to these terms with you.

Use your real name, so venues and players know who is coming.

Booking a court

When you book, your booking is between you and the venue. The venue runs the court, sets the price and its own house rules, and is responsible for the place.

Some venues confirm a booking at once. Others reply by hand within the time shown when you book. If a venue does not reply in time, the request ends and the time is free again.

Prices are shown in rupees and are set by the venue. The price you see when you book is the price of that booking.

Paying

For now you pay the venue at the venue. Sporty does not take your money.

Some venues ask for an advance: a part of the price, sent before the game to hold your time. You send it to the venue yourself and type the transfer reference in the app. If the venue does not confirm it in time, the booking ends. Any refund of an advance is between you and the venue.

Cancelling and changing time

You can cancel or move a booking for free until the time shown on the booking. A move keeps the same length and must be to a free time.

After that time, the venue's own rules apply. A venue can cancel a booking if it has to, and you will be told in the app.

Checking in

Show the check-in code from your booking at the venue's desk. A game counts as played only after it is checked in. If you do not come, the venue can mark the booking as a no-show.

Games, teams and chat

Be respectful to other players and to venues. Do not:

  • abuse, threaten or bully anyone,
  • post hate, sexual content or anything illegal,
  • send spam or ads,
  • share another person's private details,
  • pretend to be someone else.

Reviews

You can review a venue after a game you played there. Keep it honest and about your visit. We can hide a review that breaks these terms.

Reports and safety

You can report a message, review, person or venue, and block a person. Our team looks at reports. We can hide content and pause or close an account that breaks these terms.

Photos and words you add

What you add, like your picture, messages and reviews, stays yours. You let Sporty show it in the app so the app can work. Only add photos you have the right to share.

Venues on Sporty

A venue owner must have the right to list the venue, keep its courts, prices and hours correct, and honour the bookings it accepts. Our team checks each venue before it goes live. Sporty's fee for venues is agreed with each venue on its own.

Using the app fairly

Do not:

  • make fake bookings or hold times you will not use,
  • try to break into, copy or overload the app,
  • use the app in a way that breaks the law.

Play safely

Sports can cause injury. Warm up, use the right gear and follow the venue's rules. Sporty does not run the venues and is not responsible for what happens there.

Our service

We work hard to keep Sporty running and correct, but we cannot promise it will always work, or that every detail from a venue is right. We can change or stop parts of the app.

As far as the law allows, Sporty is not responsible for losses that come from using the app, from a venue, or from other players.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time: Profile, then Settings, then Delete my account. We can pause or close an account that breaks these terms.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we change the date at the top. If a change is big, we tell you in the app first. Using Sporty after a change means you accept it.

Law

These terms follow the laws of Pakistan. Any dispute goes to the courts of Karachi.
