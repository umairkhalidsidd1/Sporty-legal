Delete your Sporty account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, at any time. It takes less than a minute.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open Sporty and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete my account, then tap Delete account to confirm.

Want a copy of your data first? In the app, tap Profile, then Download my data.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign in.
  • Your name, profile picture and contact phone number.
  • Your place in teams and in games you joined.
  • Venues you saved, plans you had at venues, and your points and badges.
  • The notification tokens for your phones.

Deleting cannot be undone.

What stays, with no name on it

  • Your future bookings are cancelled, so the courts are free for others.
  • Past bookings stay with the venue for its records, marked "Deleted player".
  • Messages and reviews you wrote stay where they are, with no name on them, so chats and venue ratings still make sense.

If you run a venue

A venue has players and staff who depend on it. Hand your venue over to someone else first, then delete your account.
