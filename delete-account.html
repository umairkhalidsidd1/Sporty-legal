Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Sporty and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Edit Profile.
  3. Scroll down to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Can't open the app?

Email kicksetgoo@gmail.com from the email address you use for Sporty, with the subject Delete my Sporty account. If you signed up with a phone number, tell us that number. We reply within 7 days and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password, or your Google sign-in).
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and profile picture.

What is kept

  • Past bookings stay with the venue as its booking records, and your reviews stay on the court page. They can still show your name or email.
  • To remove your name, email and phone number from those too, email us. We remove them within 30 days.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep is kept only for as long as the law requires, then deleted.
